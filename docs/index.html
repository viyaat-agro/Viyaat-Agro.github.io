About Viyaat

Farms that run well, and make money.

Viyaat is an agriculture company working across Production, Consulting and Agri Tourism.

For four years we have grown flowers in polyhouses and horticulture crops at scale. We combine technology-led farming with hands-on management, so farms run well and make money.

What we do

Three verticals. One principle: skin in the game.

01 · Production

Polyhouse floriculture and horticulture at scale

We grow floriculture in 30+ acres of polyhouses and horticulture across 140+ acres. Protected cultivation gives us precise control over climate, water and inputs, which means consistent quality for our flowers. Our horticulture acreage gives us the scale for dependable supply.

  • Chrysanthemum

02 · Consulting

From underperforming farm to profitable farm

Many farms struggle with operations, not intent. We help farm owners operationalise their land through a plug-and-play model that covers everything, from setup to day-to-day running. We also turn underperforming farms around and take them to profitability.

We work in the structure that suits the farm: revenue share, equity or an operating partnership. We don't just advise. We have skin in the game, so our success is tied to yours.

How we work with you
  • Revenue share
  • Equity
  • Operating partnership

03 · Agri Tourism

Experience the farm

Agriculture is something people want to see for themselves. Our working farms are open to visitors, such as families, school groups, corporate teams and city dwellers. They can walk through polyhouses, see how flowers and produce are grown, take part in farm activities and connect with where food comes from.

Who visits
  • Families
  • School groups

  • City dwellers

Our Approach

  • Sustainability

    Sustainability is at the core of everything we do. We prioritize eco-friendly practices, water conservation, soil health, and biodiversity preservation to minimize our environmental footprint and promote long-term viability.

  • Innovation

    We embrace innovation and technology to optimize farming operations, enhance productivity, and improve crop resilience against climate change and other challenges.

  • Partnerships

    We believe in the power of collaboration. We work closely with researchers and industry partners to foster mutual growth, knowledge exchange, and community development. Together, we build a stronger, more resilient agricultural ecosystem.

Our Commitment

  • Quality Assurance

    Quality is our top priority. We adhere to stringent quality control measures at every stage of production, from seed selection to harvesting, processing, and packaging, ensuring that our products meet the highest standards of safety, purity, and freshness.

  • Customer Satisfaction

    We are committed to delivering exceptional value and reliable service to our wholesale, export, hospitality, events and retail partners, with consistent quality and dependable supply.

  • Social Responsibility

    Beyond business, we are dedicated to making a positive impact on society. We support local communities, promote fair practices, and contribute to initiatives that enhance food security, education, and rural development.
