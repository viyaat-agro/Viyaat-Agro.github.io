Why protected cultivation

Polyhouse farming: higher yields and climate resilience.

The technology

  1. Structure & covering

    UV poly film over a steel frame; sealed and controllable.

  2. Climate control

    Fan-pad or natural ventilation regulates temperature and humidity.

  3. Drip irrigation + fertigation

    Precise root-zone delivery of water and nutrients.

  4. Round-the-year production

    3–4 crop cycles annually vs 1–2 in the open.
